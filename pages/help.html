menu Finding your way around The extra top-bar buttons, the side menu and breadcrumbs expand_more

The top bar

  • Libraries opens a list of every library (Movies, Shows, Anime and so on). They're kept in one menu so the bar stays tidy.
  • Watchlist is your personal watch-later list. See Watchlist.
  • Requests, Calendar and Seerr are for asking for new things and seeing what's coming. See Requesting something new.
  • The palette Appearance button changes how Jellyfin looks. See Skins and colour schemes.
  • casino opens something at random. See The Random button.

The side menu

On a wide screen, the menu Open Menu button opens a side menu with your libraries, the Watchlist and Games. On phones the same menu is behind the menu icon.

Breadcrumbs

On a show's pages a trail appears at the top, such as Shows › Frieren › Season 1 › 1x01. Click any part to jump back to it. Parts with a ▾ open a quick list, so you can hop straight to another season or episode.

infoBreadcrumbs only appear when the window is wider than a tablet.
palette Skins and colour schemes Change how Jellyfin looks on this device expand_more

Click palette Appearance in the top bar and pick a Skin. Some skins also offer Color Schemes.

  • ElegantFin is the server's normal look.
  • Abyss has colour schemes: Default, Warm White, Soft Blue, Teal, Rose and Jellyfin Purple. It is designed for Jellyfin's Dark theme.
  • Default is plain Jellyfin.

The same choice is also on Settings › Display › Skin, where the gear next to it turns extra skin options on and off.

devicesYour skin is remembered by this browser, not your account. A different device or browser starts with the server's normal look.

play_circleWatching

fast_forward Skipping intros, credits and recaps The Skip button, and how to turn on recap and preview skipping expand_more

The server finds the opening, ending credits, recaps and next-episode previews in episodes and movies. When one starts, a Skip Intro or Skip Outro button appears in the bottom corner. It hides after 8 seconds; move the mouse or tap to bring it back.

Choose what happens for each part

Go to Settings › Playback › Media Segment Actions. Each kind has its own setting:

  • Ask To Skip shows the Skip button.
  • Skip skips it automatically.
  • None does nothing.
tips_and_updatesIntros and outros are set to Ask To Skip from the start. Recaps and previews are set to None, so you won't see a Skip button for them until you change those two settings.

Why there's sometimes no button

  • The first episode of a season usually has no Skip Intro button. That's on purpose, so you see the opening at least once.
  • Detection works by spotting the same music across episodes. Shows whose opening changes a lot, or one-off specials, may have no button or a slightly wrong one.
pause_circle The pause screen The information screen that appears when you pause expand_more

Pause and leave it for 5 seconds, and a screen appears with the title, plot, how much you've watched, and what time it will finish (Ends at).

  • Click or tap anywhere, or press Space or Enter, to carry on watching.
  • Esc or close closes it and stays paused.
  • arrow_back leaves the video.

live_tvShows and anime

video_library A show's page The episode row, the Next Up badge and switching seasons expand_more

A show's page lists its episodes directly, already scrolled to where you are:

  • The episode you should watch next has a Next Up badge in its corner.
  • For shows with several seasons, the Select Season button next to the row title switches season.
  • The grid_view button (hover over the row title) shows every episode at once as a grid instead of a scrolling row.

Specials that belong in a particular spot are placed in the season where they air. See Specials.

star Specials, OVAs and movies in a show Where specials appear, and why watching one marks another expand_more
  • All of a show's specials are in its Specials season.
  • Specials that should be watched at a certain point also appear in that season's episode list, e.g. after the finale or between two episodes.
  • Some specials are grouped into their own collection, with a breadcrumb trail back to the show they belong to.

Watched in one place, watched everywhere

Some movies exist in two places, for example in Movies and also as a show's special. Watching either copy marks both as watched (and keeps your resume point in step), so it won't show up as unwatched in the other place.

Tags

A title's details list a Tags line. Click any tag to see everything else with it.

Want bonus specials moved or hidden? See Alternate Order.

low_priority Alternate Order Move bonus specials to the end of each season, or hide them expand_more

Some anime have lots of bonus features: talk shows, gag shorts and recaps that would otherwise play between story episodes. Alternate Order keeps them out of the way.

ModeWhat happens
low_priority OffThe normal order. Bonus specials play where they aired.
low_priority ReorderBonus specials play at the end of their season, after the last episode.
visibility_off HiddenBonus specials are skipped. They're still in the Specials season if you want one.

Change it for one show

On the show's page (or any of its seasons), click the Alternate Order button just after the favorite_border heart. Each click moves Off → Reorder → Hidden → Off. Hover over it to see which mode it's in; it turns blue when it's on.

The button only appears on shows that have bonus features. That's mostly anime.

Change it for every show

Go to Settings › Display › Alternate Order and pick Default for every series: Disabled by default, Alternate order or Hide bonus features. It saves straight away.

What follows your choice

  • Autoplay and the next-episode queue.
  • Season pages and their Play button.
  • Next Up and Continue Watching. With Reorder, a show you've finished can come back to Next Up for its bonus specials.
warningHide bonus features occasionally hides something with a bit of story in it. The Specials season itself is never changed.

In the cast row

On a show, season, episode or movie page, each actor in Cast & Crew has the character they play right before them. If they play several, the characters share one card in tiles; hover over a tile to see its name.

On a show's page, the cast only includes people from seasons up to the one you're on, so actors from later seasons don't spoil anything.

Character pages

Click a character for their page: picture, description (Show more for the rest), Voiced by, Appears in and links to AniList, AniDB and MyAnimeList.

visibilityWords that would spoil the story are blacked out. Click one to reveal it.

An actor's own page also gets a Characters row listing everyone they've played.

Your settings

Under Settings › Display › Characters. Changes save straight away and show on the next page you open.

Series castWhich seasons' cast a show's page shows: first, current, all, or Progressive (up to the season you're on; the normal setting).
Preferred character namesJapanese names, English names, or English (Weebified), which uses the names fans use, like "Sensei".
Character sourceFor anime: AniList (one picture per character) or AniDB (each title's own version of the character).
Spoiler versionsWith AniDB: show spoiler versions of a character only once you've watched that far, or never.

Reality and game shows also get a Season X cast row with that season's contestants.

blur_on Spoiler Guard Blur pictures and hide details of episodes you haven't seen expand_more

Turn Spoiler Guard on for a show and every episode you haven't watched yet has its picture blurred and its details hidden. Once you watch an episode, it shows normally.

Turning it on

On a show, movie or collection page, click blur_off (Spoiler Guard: Off) in the row of buttons, just before more_horiz. It changes to blur_on Spoiler Guard: On.

  • On a collection it protects every movie inside it.
  • For something not on the server yet, open it from search and use Enable Spoiler Guard; it switches on when the title arrives.
  • The button is on the show's page, not on season or episode pages.

What gets hidden

  • Episode pictures are blurred, and so are the preview pictures when you drag along the progress bar.
  • Episode titles become Season 1, Episode 5, and descriptions read Spoiler Guard activated.
  • Air dates, ratings, guest stars, chapter names and tags are hidden.
  • Posters for later seasons stay blurred until you start that season.

The next episode you're due to watch, and the rest of that season, keep their titles so you can find your place. Their descriptions stay hidden.

devicesSpoiler Guard works in every Jellyfin app, not just the browser, and follows your account.

Choosing what's hidden

Press ? to open the Jellyfin Enhanced panel and find Spoiler Guard. Under Show me this even with Spoiler Guard on, untick anything you don't mind seeing, e.g. Episode titles or Cast list.

refreshAfter turning it on or off, pictures change straight away but titles and descriptions may need you to leave the page and come back.

  • On a title's page: click the same button next to Trailer.

On a playlist's page

push_pin Pinning to the Home screen Put any title, collection or library on your Home screen expand_more
  1. Open the more_vert menu on a poster (or right-click it) and choose push_pin Pin to Home Screen.
  2. Pick a list to add it to. Pinned is the default, or choose New... to start another row. Then press Pin.

For a collection, library, show, genre, actor and so on, you can instead choose Pin Items from …. That gives you a whole Home row of what's inside it, e.g. a row of every movie in a collection.

To unpin, hover over the pinned poster and click push_pin Unpin from Home Screen; the arrows beside it move it left or right. A message offers Undo if you change your mind.

homeThe Home screen

dashboard_customize Rearranging the Home screen Hide, move and restyle the rows on your Home screen expand_more

Your Home screen is yours: changes here don't affect anyone else, and they follow your account to other devices.

From the row itself

Hover over a row's title and three buttons fade in (on a phone, tap more_vert instead):

refreshRefresh: load fresh items.
grid_viewGrid layout / Row layout: show everything at once instead of scrolling.
settingsConfigure: move the row up or down (hold the arrow to send it to the top or bottom), hide it, or change how its posters look.

When you hide a row, a message offers Undo.

All rows at once

Settings › Home › Home Sections Order lists every row. Drag or use the arrows to reorder, and use the switches to turn rows on and off. Hidden rows can be brought back here.

Restore Defaults puts everything back the way it started.

auto_awesome Recommendation and seasonal rows The rows that keep appearing as you scroll down expand_more

Keep scrolling down the Home screen and more rows load by themselves, picked for you:

  • Because you watched …, Because you finished …, Because you liked …
  • Because … is on your Watchlist
  • Genre, studio and collection rows, such as Top Rated Comedy Movies or Shows from MAPPA

They change each visit. Use a row's refresh to shuffle it.

Seasonal rows

Around holidays, extra rows appear by themselves: Halloween in October, Thanksgiving in late November, Christmas in December.

view_carousel The banners The slideshow at the top and the big spotlight rows expand_more

The slideshow at the top

It shows new anime and anime movies from the last week that you haven't watched yet, and changes every 10 seconds.

  • Play starts it; the other buttons open its page, heart it, or add it to your Watchlist.
  • Use the arrows (or swipe on a phone) to move between slides, and pause to stop it moving. With a keyboard: ← → and Space.

If you've watched everything new, the slideshow may have very little in it.

Spotlight rows

Some Home rows show one big title at a time instead of posters. Each has play_arrow Play, a Watchlist button, and info to open its page. It pauses while your mouse is over it.

travel_exploreFinding and requesting

add_circle Requesting something new Ask for a movie or show that isn't here, and track your requests expand_more

Making a request

  1. Search for it as usual. Below the results from the server, a Discover on Seerr row shows matching titles that aren't here yet. You can also browse the Seerr tab in the top bar.
  2. Press Request. For a show, pick which seasons you want.
  3. Once it's downloaded, it appears on the server by itself.

Other places you can request from:

  • Request More next to a show's Seasons, when there are seasons the server doesn't have.
  • The Recommended row on a title's page, which suggests things not on the server yet.
  • A collection's page, which lists the movies in that series that are missing.
infoThere may be a limit on how many requests you can make; your remaining requests are shown next to the button.

Tracking your requests

The Requests tab in the top bar shows:

DownloadsWhat's downloading now, with a progress bar.
RequestsYour requests and their status, from Pending Approval to Available. Available ones get a play_arrow button.
IssuesProblems you've reported.
HistoryWhat's been added recently.

Reporting a problem

If something's wrong with a title (bad video, missing audio, wrong subtitles), use the Report issue button on its page. Choose the kind of problem, and the season and episode if it's a show.
