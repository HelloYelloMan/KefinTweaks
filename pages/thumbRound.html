Thumbnail Round

Each round is a random season you have watched some of. For every episode of it you have watched, pick the thumbnail that suits it best from its stills, or keep the one it has.

An episode you have decided on, here or in the metadata viewer, does not come up again.

No spoilers within the season

A thumbnail must not spoil the season's story at face value: someone who has not reached the episode should learn nothing from it. If it shows a story element, what it means should only become clear once you have watched up to that episode.

The rule covers the episode's own season. Later seasons can show anything from earlier ones: a character revealed by surprise in season 1 is just a character in season 2.

What makes a good thumbnail

It depends on the episode, and there is no hard rule. These are the common kinds:

Character
One of the episode's main characters. The safe choice.
Context
Interesting at a glance, but only makes full sense once you have watched the episodes before it.
Setting
Where the episode takes place: a new town, the festival, the beach.
Premise
The situation the episode opens with, never how it turns out.
Pairing
The two or three characters the episode is about.
Mood
A frame chosen for its atmosphere: rain on a window, a sunset, an empty room.
Gag
An exaggerated reaction or a comic frame.
Object
A prop that matters in the episode: a letter, a ticket, a strange device.

Avoid

  • Reveals: an identity, a transformation, a power-up, a death, a confession and its answer.
  • A character whose first appearance is a twist.
  • The episode's ending or cliffhanger.
  • Credits and on-screen text, fades, motion blur, mid-blink faces, heavy letterboxing.

Controls

  • Click a still to pick it and move on. Hover to see it large.
  • ← → move between episodes. Space keeps the current thumbnail.
  • 1–9 pick that still. Backspace clears the pick.
  • After the last episode every pick is shown on its episode. Submit writes the picks and records the keeps; nothing is changed before that.
